## 👋 About Me

Hello! I'm a developer and lifelong learner passionate about building elegant solutions to complex problems. Whether I'm deep-coding in a terminal or exploring the latest in AI, I find joy in the process of creation.

This blog serves as my **Digital Garden**—a place where I plant ideas, nurture projects, and document my growth as a creator.

### What to Expect Here

I’ll be using this space to share:
* **Project Breakdowns:** The "how" and "why" behind my builds.
* **Technical Deep Dives:** Exploring interesting concepts in software and math.
* **Progress Logs:** My journey through new languages and frameworks.

### The Foundation

I believe that the best way to learn is to explain. As I document my findings, I'll be using clear logic and the occasional formula to keep things precise. For instance, I'm a big believer in the **Compound Interest** of learning:

$$Knowledge = (Initial \text{ } Intel) \times (1 + r)^n$$

Where $r$ is the daily rate of learning and $n$ is the number of days spent consistently building.
