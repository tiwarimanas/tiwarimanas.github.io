This is the beginning. Every journey starts somewhere, and for this blog — this quiet little corner of the internet — it starts here.

## Why Write?

Writing is thinking made visible. When I code, I think in abstractions, in functions and data flows. But when I write, I think in *meaning*. The two practices complement each other in surprising ways.

> "The act of writing is the act of discovering what you believe." — David Hare

## What to Expect

I'll write about things that interest me:

- **Software craft** — patterns, tools, and the philosophy behind clean code
- **Learning in public** — documenting what I'm figuring out as I go
- **Small observations** — the kind of things you notice when you slow down

## The Setup

This blog is deliberately simple. Markdown files rendered by Jekyll. No complex build steps, no heavy frameworks. Just words on a page.
